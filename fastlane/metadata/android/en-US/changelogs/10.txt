Kairn 0.8.0

- Read aloud has a sleep timer: 15, 30 or 45 minutes, or the end of the chapter.
- Select a passage to share it, or look a word up in the dictionary app you already have.
- Kindle highlights come in from My Clippings.txt, each quote placed where it actually is in the book.
- A "Continue reading" shelf of the books you were last in.
- Your reading: minutes a day, a streak, and the books you have finished, kept on the phone.
- Kairn can update itself now: one check a day, with a switch in Settings.
