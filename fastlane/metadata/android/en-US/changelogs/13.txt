Reading aloud keeps going with the screen off.

- Read aloud now survives a locked screen, with a Stop button in the notification.
- Continue reading shows progress and when you were last in the book, with one progress bar.
- Automatic backup: pick a folder once, and a backup lands there every few days.
- Searching the text inside books is cached, so a repeat search is quick.
- The device scan no longer runs on every open; "Find books on this device" still forces it.
- Settings shows the size of the search cache and can clear it.
- After restoring a backup your books come back without needing a rescan.
- Built against Android 16, with current libraries.
