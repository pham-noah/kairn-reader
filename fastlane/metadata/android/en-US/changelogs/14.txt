Fixed and faster.

- After restoring a backup, or updating, your books come back without a rescan.
- The device scan no longer runs on every open; if a book's file is missing it runs on its own, at most once an hour.
- A settings file written by another version no longer resets your theme, folders and onboarding; a copy of the old file is kept.
- The search cache no longer keeps entries for books you removed, and its size is measured off the main thread.
- Searching your library stays quick on a large shelf.
