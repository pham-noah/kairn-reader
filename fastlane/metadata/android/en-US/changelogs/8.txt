0.7.0

- Read aloud: the chapter you are in, spoken from the place you are at, carrying on into the next chapter when it runs out.
- Search inside your books: the library search now looks through the text of every book, not just titles.
- Auto scroll: the page moves by itself, at a speed you set for that book.
- Pick your typeface: default, serif, sans or mono.
- Custom page and ink colours, laid over any theme, remembered per book.
- Six highlight colours instead of four.
- Reading speed, so "min left" uses your own pace instead of a fixed guess.
- Grey is a real charcoal now instead of a pale mid-grey.
- The sort arrow no longer sits on top of the sort label.
