Kairn reads the EPUBs you already own. Open a book from your storage, or point Kairn at a folder and it
picks up new ones as they arrive. There is no account to make and no library to import into. Your place,
your highlights and notes, and your settings all live on the phone, in plain files you could read yourself.

A book is recognised by what is inside it rather than by its file name, so renaming it or moving it to
another folder brings your place and your highlights along.

Reading
Page or scroll, whichever you prefer, and auto scroll moves the page by itself at a speed you set. Text size,
spacing, margins, justification and hyphenation are set per book. Three typefaces ship inside the app -
Literata, Manrope and Libron - along with the book's own font, so nothing is downloaded to render a page.
Five page tones from light to true black, a warm filter for late nights, and brightness that either applies
to Kairn alone or follows the rest of the phone. The slider at the bottom shows where you are and roughly
how long is left.

Read aloud
Kairn can speak the book with the voice your phone already has, at the speed you like. A sleep timer stops
it after fifteen, thirty or forty five minutes, or at the end of the chapter, so it can read you to sleep
without reading all night.

Highlights and notes
Six highlight colours, notes you type on a selection, and one bookmark per chapter. Select a passage to copy
it, share it with another app, or look a word up in the dictionary you already have. Each book keeps a list
you can jump from, and it can be exported as Markdown.

Already reading on a Kindle
If you have a My Clippings.txt file from a Kindle, Kairn brings those highlights in and puts each quote on
the page where it actually sits.

Search
Search the whole text of the book you are reading.

Sending books over Wi-Fi
Kairn shows you an address. Open it in a browser on your computer on the same network and drop EPUB files onto
the page. A four digit code is part of the address, so nobody else on the network can upload, and the
transfer stops as soon as you leave the screen.

Backup
One zip with everything in it, and you can include the book files too. Point Kairn at a folder once and it
writes that zip every few days on its own, keeping the newest few. Restoring checks the archive before it
touches anything, takes a safety copy first, and can either merge with or replace what is on the phone.

Your reading
A quiet record of the days you read, how long for, and how many books you have finished. A Continue reading
shelf puts the book you are in the middle of back in front of you, with how far in you are and when you were
last in it.

Languages
English, Español, Suomi, 日本語, Tiếng Việt and 简体中文, switched from Settings without leaving the app.

Privacy
No analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts, no ads. The one connection Kairn makes on its own is the
update check, and only in builds that ship from GitHub - a store build has no update check at all. The other
piece of network code is the Wi-Fi transfer, and even that only listens: the browser on your computer makes
the request, and the server stops when you leave the screen.

Android 8.0 or newer. Apache-2.0. Built on the Readium Kotlin Toolkit and AndroidX.
