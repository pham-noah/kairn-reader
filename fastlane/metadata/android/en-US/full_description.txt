Kairn is an offline EPUB reader for Android. It reads the books you already own and keeps everything about
them - your place, highlights, notes and settings - on your phone.

NO ACCOUNT, NOTHING TO SET UP
No sign-up, no subscription, no library to import into. Open an EPUB from your storage, or point Kairn at
a folder and let it watch for new books.

LIBRARY
Grid or list, search and sort, reading status, and collections and tags you can rename or delete. Books
are identified by their contents, so moving or renaming a file keeps its highlights and your place.

READING
Page or scroll mode. Per-book text size, line and paragraph spacing, margins, justification and
hyphenation. Five page tones from light to true black, a warm-light filter, and brightness that affects
this app only or follows the system. A whole-book progress slider with a time-left estimate.

SEND BOOKS OVER WI-FI
Kairn shows an address; open it in a browser on a computer on the same network and drop EPUB files on the
page. A four-digit code is part of the address, so nobody else on the network can upload, and the transfer
stops when you leave the screen.

HIGHLIGHTS AND NOTES
Four highlight colours, notes you type on a selection, and one bookmark per chapter. A per-book list to
jump from, and Markdown export.

SEARCH
Full text inside the book you are reading.

BACKUP
One zip with everything, optionally including the book files. A restore validates the archive first, takes
a safety copy, and can merge with or replace what is on the phone.

PRIVACY
No analytics, no crash reporting, no accounts and no ads. Nothing touches the network unless you start the
Wi-Fi transfer, and even then Kairn only listens - the browser on your computer makes the request, and the
server stops when you leave the screen. The app opens no outgoing connection. Your data lives in plain JSON
files inside the app.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer. Apache-2.0, built on the Readium Kotlin Toolkit and AndroidX.
