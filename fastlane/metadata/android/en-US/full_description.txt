Kairn is an EPUB reader for Android, built for the books you already own.

No account, no network permission, no analytics. Your library, reading positions, highlights and settings
live in plain JSON files inside the app, and a single zip backup moves them to another device.

LIBRARY
Grid or list, search, sort, collections, tags and reading status. Watch the folders you choose, or let
Kairn find the EPUBs already on your device.

READING
Page or scroll mode. Per-book text size, line and paragraph spacing, margins, justification and
hyphenation. Light, dark, sepia and true-black themes, a warm-light filter, and brightness that affects
this app only.

NAVIGATION
Tap zones that follow the book's reading direction, optional volume-key page turns, a table of contents,
and a chapter progress slider with a time-left estimate based on the chapter's real word count.

HIGHLIGHTS AND NOTES
Four highlight colours, bookmarks, a per-book list you can jump from, and Markdown export.

SEARCH
Full-text search inside a book.

BACKUP
One zip containing everything, with a safety copy taken before anything is replaced, so a restore cannot
leave you with less than you started with.

Requires Android 8.0 or newer.
