# Kairn 1.1.0

## A release about the two ways people read

1.1.0 is the first release since 1.0.0 that adds things rather than taking them away. Most of it is the screen
you read on; a good part of it is the thing that reads to you.

## Four voices, heard before they are chosen

The cast sheet asked a reader to hand a character a "Voice 2" and hope. Four chips, four numbers, no way to
know what any of them sounded like without starting the chapter, waiting for that character to speak, and
living with the result.

The sheet now opens with four pads. Each one says the same sentence in one of the four slot voices - lower
narrator, second voice, second voice higher, narrator higher - so the four are comparable by ear. Tap one while
the chapter is reading and the chapter stops, the sample plays, and the reading resumes from the word it
stopped on; the page never moves.

## Read aloud, from the lock screen

The notification that keeps Kairn alive with the screen off used to say "Reading aloud" and offer one button:
Stop. It never named the book or the chapter, and it could not pause.

It is now a transport notification: book on the first line, chapter on the second, and four actions -
previous chapter, pause or resume, next chapter, stop - on the lock screen, in a car, and on a Bluetooth
headset's own button, through a media session the reader publishes to. Pausing keeps the notification, because
a notification that disappears on pause cannot offer a resume.

## This page, for every book

The reading settings are per book, and they stay per book. But a reader who has just got the margins, the
typeface and the line spacing right for one novel does not want to do it again for the next nine.

The display sheet ends with "Apply to every book" under "Reset this book to your defaults". It writes the
current settings to every record through the same queue as a single change, so a library of forty books is
not forty writes.

## Also in this release

- A reading goal and a streak, on the stats sheet and in Settings.
- A night schedule: the theme and the page colours follow the clock if you ask them to.
- Series editing and sorting, for a shelf that arrives from a catalog out of order.
- Larger type, drawn - every sheet in the app now holds up at 1.3x.
- A tablet pass: two-page reading, a wider shelf, and sheets that use the width.
- Fixes to the shelf and the backup screen, and a grid-and-sort setting that a round trip through backup
  used to drop.
