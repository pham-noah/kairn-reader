What changed from round 3, and why.

    Liquid Glass surface

    • The tab bar floats as one glass capsule. The current tab sits in a lighter glass pill.
    • Buttons, chips and cards are translucent. Book covers and the soft colour behind them show through as you scroll.
    • Sheets and menus float with rounded corners and a heavier blur.
    • In the reader, the top buttons, title and bottom bar float over the page. Light and sepia pages get light glass.
    • Panels turn opaque when your phone has reduce transparency on, or when Surface is set to Solid.
